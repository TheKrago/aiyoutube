Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

AiYoutube Clipper ("the tool") is a private desktop application used only by its owner.

Data from TikTok

When the owner logs in with TikTok, the tool receives an access token and the account's display name (scopes user.info.basic and video.upload). The token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is used only to upload the owner's videos to the owner's own TikTok account and to check their upload status.

What we don't do

The tool does not collect data about other TikTok users, does not sell or share any data, does not use analytics or advertising, and has no servers. Nothing is sent anywhere except to TikTok's official API.

Deleting data

Deleting the tool's data folder removes the stored token. Access can also be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings > Security > Manage app permissions.
