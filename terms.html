AiYoutube Clipper ("the tool") is a private desktop application run by its owner for their own TikTok account. It is not available to other users.

What the tool does

The tool creates short video clips on the owner's computer and, after the owner logs in with TikTok, uploads them to the owner's TikTok account as drafts using TikTok's official Content Posting API. Nothing is posted publicly without the owner finishing the post in the TikTok app.

All use of TikTok through the tool follows the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. The owner is responsible for the content they choose to post and for crediting the original creators.
